## Why

The pergola needs a durable, level wooden deck that creates a seamless transition from the French doors while protecting the structure from ground moisture, vegetation growth, and movement caused by freeze-thaw cycles. Planning the foundation, drainage clearances, and material choices up front reduces the risk of rot, uneven boards, blocked pergola drain outlets, and premature repairs.

## What Changes

- Define a deck project plan for a wooden terrace slightly larger than the 5800 mm x 3100 mm pergola base.
- Specify a stable layered foundation using leveled subgrade, geotextile, gravel, concrete garden tiles, and adjustable plastic pedestal supports.
- Account for the existing pergola geometry, including three anthracite aluminium legs on the garden side and drain outlets in two legs discharging outward from the pergola.
- Establish requirements for slope handling, vegetation suppression, drainage, long-term maintainability, and compatibility with Czech climate and common Czech building-material suppliers.
- Include material and sourcing considerations for suppliers such as Artisan, Bauhaus, DEK, or equivalent local trade shops.
- Use the reference photos in `materials/examples/bgauhause_photos/` as example material inputs for board species, board dimensions, spacers, and terrace screws.
