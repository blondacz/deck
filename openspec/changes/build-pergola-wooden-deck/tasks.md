- [ ] 3.1 Remove grass, loose organic material, and unstable soil from the full deck footprint.
- [ ] 3.2 Excavate sandy soil only as needed to correct slope and fit the planned foundation build-up.
- [ ] 3.3 Keep excavation clear of existing concrete pergola pads unless pad depth and stability are confirmed.
- [ ] 3.4 Shape and compact the subgrade so water drains away from the house and does not collect below timber.
- [ ] 3.5 Install geotextile across the prepared footprint with adequate overlaps and edge coverage.
