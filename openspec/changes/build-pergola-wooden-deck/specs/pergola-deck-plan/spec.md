## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Deck footprint and transition
The deck plan SHALL define a wooden deck footprint slightly larger than the 5800 mm x 3100 mm pergola base and SHALL provide a practical, level transition from the French doors to the pergola area.

#### Scenario: Footprint is set from site measurements
- **WHEN** the final deck outline is marked on site
- **THEN** the marked footprint extends beyond the pergola base where needed for the door transition and walking comfort while preserving access around pergola legs and drainage outlets

#### Scenario: Finished height is checked against doors
- **WHEN** the finished deck height is calculated
- **THEN** it accounts for threshold clearance, decking board thickness, joist height, pedestal adjustment range, and safe door operation

#### Scenario: Threshold transition is detailed
- **WHEN** the deck connection at the French doors is designed
- **THEN** it includes a replaceable transition board or equivalent detail that creates a comfortable step while preserving drainage, ventilation, and facade inspection access

#### Scenario: Existing height constraints are used
- **WHEN** the deck build-up is calculated
- **THEN** the plan uses the known approximate levels of 200 mm from turf to French door threshold and 100 mm from concrete pads to threshold as constraints to validate joist, board, tile, gravel, and support heights

### Requirement: Stable layered foundation
The deck foundation SHALL use leveled and compacted ground, geotextile, drainage gravel, concrete bearing elements, and either adjustable plastic supports or fixed frame spacer pads to provide a stable and serviceable base.

#### Scenario: Sloped ground is prepared
- **WHEN** ground preparation begins
- **THEN** loose organic material is removed, the base is shaped for drainage, and the subgrade is compacted before geotextile is installed

#### Scenario: Sandy soil is excavated safely
- **WHEN** sandy soil is removed to level the deck base
- **THEN** excavation is limited to the depth required for the foundation build-up and does not undermine existing concrete pergola pads or house-side support conditions

#### Scenario: Vegetation growth is suppressed
- **WHEN** the foundation layers are installed
- **THEN** geotextile and concrete garden tiles cover the deck area sufficiently to inhibit grass and weed growth below the timber structure

#### Scenario: Supports carry the joist grid
- **WHEN** adjustable plastic supports or fixed frame spacer pads are positioned
- **THEN** each support sits on a stable concrete tile or flat-laid garden edging block bearing point and creates a level joist structure within the support manufacturer's load and height limits

#### Scenario: Edge pavers retain gravel and soil
- **WHEN** the deck perimeter is prepared next to turf or loose sandy soil
- **THEN** concrete grass pavers such as 600 mm x 400 mm x 80 mm DITON `zatravnovaci dlazdice` may be used as hidden perimeter bearing and edge-retaining elements to reduce grass and soil migration into the gravel layer

#### Scenario: Edge pavers support fascia studs
- **WHEN** edge studs or fascia support points are fixed near the perimeter
- **THEN** they may bear on hidden perimeter grass pavers if the fixing lands in a solid web section and does not crack the paver

#### Scenario: Internal supports use solid bearing elements
- **WHEN** internal frame support points are installed
- **THEN** they use flat-laid garden edging blocks or concrete tiles rather than grass pavers unless the grass paver bearing area and fixing method are verified for the selected support

#### Scenario: Supports are secured to concrete bearing elements
- **WHEN** supports or frame spacer pads are installed on concrete tiles or flat-laid garden edging blocks
- **THEN** they are mechanically fixed with compatible exterior screws, plugs, or anchors where required by the product and without cracking the bearing element

### Requirement: Drainage and moisture control
The deck plan SHALL keep water moving away from timber, the house facade, and pergola leg fixings, and SHALL keep existing pergola gutter drain outlets unobstructed.

#### Scenario: Pergola leg drains remain functional
- **WHEN** joists, decking boards, and perimeter finishes are laid out around the two drain-outlet legs
- **THEN** the outlets discharge freely outside the pergola and remain visible or accessible for inspection and cleaning

#### Scenario: Pergola documentation is used
- **WHEN** the layout is checked against existing pergola constraints
- **THEN** the plan uses the pergola documentation values including 5800 mm width, 3100 mm depth, 2660 mm pass-through height, 120 mm x 120 mm posts, three posts, and documented water outlet positions

#### Scenario: Timber avoids standing water
- **WHEN** the deck structure is installed
- **THEN** timber members have ventilation clearance from the ground and are not in direct contact with soil, gravel fines, or standing water

#### Scenario: Water flow is tested
- **WHEN** the foundation and drainage paths are complete but before final closure of perimeter details
- **THEN** water poured through the pergola drains and across likely rain paths exits without pooling under structural timber

### Requirement: Exterior-rated timber and fixings
The deck plan SHALL use exterior-rated timber, composite, or thermally modified boards and corrosion-resistant fixings suitable for Czech outdoor climate.

#### Scenario: Materials are selected for exterior exposure
- **WHEN** deck boards, joists, supports, and fasteners are procured
- **THEN** each selected product is rated for exterior use, moisture exposure, and expected freeze-thaw conditions

#### Scenario: Fasteners resist corrosion
- **WHEN** boards and structural members are fixed
- **THEN** fasteners are stainless steel or manufacturer-approved exterior coated fasteners compatible with the selected decking material

#### Scenario: Board spacing is controlled
- **WHEN** deck boards are installed
- **THEN** spacer pads or a spacing tool are used to maintain consistent manufacturer-compatible gaps between boards

### Requirement: Maintainability and inspection
The deck plan SHALL allow future inspection, cleaning, re-leveling, and replacement of critical parts without dismantling the entire deck.

#### Scenario: Three exposed sides are covered with fascia
- **WHEN** perimeter finishing is installed
- **THEN** three exposed sides of the deck are covered with fascia boards while preserving ventilation and access to drain/service points

#### Scenario: Fascia board surface is selected
- **WHEN** fascia material is procured
- **THEN** purpose-made smooth fascia boards or compatible non-grooved boards are preferred over grooved terrace boards for the vertical face

#### Scenario: Service points remain accessible
- **WHEN** perimeter boards, fascia, or trim are designed
- **THEN** access is retained to pergola drain outlets, key adjustable supports, and areas where leaves or sediment are likely to accumulate

#### Scenario: Deck can be re-leveled
- **WHEN** minor settlement or seasonal movement occurs after installation
- **THEN** adjustable supports can be accessed or exposed locally so the affected deck section can be corrected

### Requirement: Supplier-neutral procurement
The deck plan SHALL identify material categories and performance requirements that can be sourced from Czech trade shops such as Artisan, Bauhaus, DEK, or equivalent suppliers.

#### Scenario: Supplier options are compared
- **WHEN** procurement is prepared
- **THEN** comparable products are evaluated by dimensions, exterior rating, load capacity, adjustment range, warranty, availability, and compatibility rather than by brand alone

#### Scenario: Product substitutions preserve requirements
- **WHEN** a preferred product is unavailable
- **THEN** the replacement product meets the same functional requirements for durability, drainage, support, and maintainability

#### Scenario: Reference photos are used for comparison
- **WHEN** the material shortlist is prepared
- **THEN** the Bauhaus example photos in `materials/examples/bgauhause_photos/` are reviewed for candidate board species, board dimensions, spacer pads, and terrace screws before final supplier comparison
